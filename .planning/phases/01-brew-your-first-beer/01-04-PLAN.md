3. Brewer stats (all Int; fixed 5-gallon batch):
   - Attenuation chain (permille), applied in this attribution order: reference a0 = 750 (attributed to the strength band); then grain-bill sugar (Σ percent × attenuationPermillePerPercent for grain-bill adjuncts, in entry order); then mash band delta; then (yeast.attenuationPermille − 750); then ferment-temp band delta; then fermentDuration band delta. The running value is clamped to 550...950 at the end; any clamp adjustment is its own step attributed to the yeast with detail "attenuationLimit".
   - `fg(a) = ogPoints − divRound((ogPoints − 1000) × a, 1000)`; `abv(a) = divRound((ogPoints − fg(a)) × 13125, 10000)` (tenths of a percent). stats.fgPoints = fg(final), stats.abvTenths = abv(final).
   - ABV attribute rows by telescoping marginal attribution: the strength row's delta = abv(a0); each later step's row delta = abv(a_i) − abv(a_{i−1}). The sum of rows therefore equals abv(final) exactly, with no remainder rule. Omit zero-delta rows. Emit these rows in the global stage order (fermentable rows for sugar, then strength, mash, yeast, ferment temp, duration), whatever order they were computed in. The ABV attribute is 1:1 with ABV percent (5.1% → 5.1), and the usual cap row applies above 10.0.
   - SRM: per grain-bill entry, `lbsTenths = divRound(strength.grainLbsTenths × percent, 100)`; `mcuTenths = divRound(Σ lovibondTenths × lbsTenths, 50)`; stats.srmTenths = morey.srmTenths(mcuTenths).
