## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim): "A player installs Brew Fighter from TestFlight on their iPhone and brews a beer whose 16 sensory attributes, glass, and style label come entirely from their own recipe choices, and the beer is still there the next time they open the app."

<objective>
Let the player compose their own recipe instead of brewing the starter as-is: the stage wizard (D-01/D-02) with the pre-seeded house pale ale and Clear (D-06), the Review screen hosting BREW, and the Mash (grain bill in percent, batch strength, mash temp) and Boil (hops by slot and amount) stages built from chunky custom controls.

Purpose: BREW-01 composition, one-handed and portrait, with every edit routed through the engine's RecipeRules.
Output: Wizard shell, Review, Mash, Boil, reusable UI-kit controls (IngredientCard, StepperButton, BandDial, Panel), catalog strings, wizard UI tests.
</objective>
