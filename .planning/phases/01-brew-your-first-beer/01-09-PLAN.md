## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim): "A player installs Brew Fighter from TestFlight on their iPhone and brews a beer whose 16 sensory attributes, glass, and style label come entirely from their own recipe choices, and the beer is still there the next time they open the app."

<objective>
Move the project onto the locked toolchain: a human upgrades macOS and Xcode (the only thing Claude cannot do), then the app's deployment target is raised to the locked iOS 26.0 floor, the full suite is re-proven on an iOS 26+ simulator, the Xcode 26+ concurrency settings are adopted where the new Xcode recognizes them, and the app gains a 4+-appropriate generated icon needed for upload.

Purpose: PLAT-05 requires building on the current SDK (App Store uploads have required Xcode 26 / iOS 26 SDK since 2026-04-28), and ROADMAP criterion 5's pseudo-localized run belongs on the final SDK.
Output: Upgraded toolchain (human), iOS 26 project settings, a green full suite on iOS 26+, the app icon.

Note on task order: the human-action gate comes first because no command in this plan can run on Xcode 16.4. The tracer is the first implementation task.
</objective>
