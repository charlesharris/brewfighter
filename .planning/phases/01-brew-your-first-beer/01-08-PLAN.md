## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim): "A player installs Brew Fighter from TestFlight on their iPhone and brews a beer whose 16 sensory attributes, glass, and style label come entirely from their own recipe choices, and the beer is still there the next time they open the app."

Make the beer inspectable and the brew moment satisfying: the beer detail with family-grouped attribute bars and nerd stats, the per-attribute contribution ledger sheet, the procedurally drawn glass (animated hero, static Cellar thumbnails), and the staged, skippable brew reveal.

Purpose: Completes ROADMAP criterion 3 (identical, inspectable beers with a visibly distinct glass and a ledger behind every attribute) and the phase's signature juice moment (D-09).
Output: BeerDetailView, AttributeFamilyBars, NerdStatsPanel, LedgerSheet, GlassView, BrewRevealView, the Cellar grid, catalog strings, UI tests.
</objective>
