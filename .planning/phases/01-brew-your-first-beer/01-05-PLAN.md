## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim): "A player installs Brew Fighter from TestFlight on their iPhone and brews a beer whose 16 sensory attributes, glass, and style label come entirely from their own recipe choices, and the beer is still there the next time they open the app."

<objective>
Finish PLAT-03: a single validated decode path for every save, export through the share sheet and import back through the file picker with a confirmed, backed-up replace, and the frozen golden-save fixture for schema version 1 that later phases must migrate from.

Purpose: Imports are the phase's only untrusted input. The golden fixture turns "any schema change ships a migration" (standing rule) into a failing test.
Output: SaveValidator, SaveMigrator, content-aware SaveCodec.decode, store export/import intents, the Save Transfer sheet, golden fixture and tests.
</objective>
