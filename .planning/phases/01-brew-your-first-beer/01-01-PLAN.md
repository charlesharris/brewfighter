## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 1 Goal (verbatim; not yet in user-story form, see note): "A player installs Brew Fighter from TestFlight on their iPhone and brews a beer whose 16 sensory attributes, glass, and style label come entirely from their own recipe choices, and the beer is still there the next time they open the app."

Note (MVP mode): the Goal line is not in "As a / I want to / so that" form. The planner did not invent a story; run `/gsd-mvp-phase 1` if the story framing is wanted.

<assumption_delta_decision>
decision: no-change
rationale: The detector fired on prose terms only ("optional art spike", "style label never chosen by the player", "custom chrome"); none is a singular-to-plural identity transition, and this is the project's first phase with no pre-existing identity model to migrate.
</assumption_delta_decision>

<objective>
Build the engine half of the Walking Skeleton: the `BrewCore` Swift package that turns a recipe into a deterministic beer (16 attributes, additive contribution ledger, computed style), commits it through the reducer (batch number, day counter), and round-trips the whole `GameState` through a versioned JSON save envelope. Everything is verified headlessly with `swift test` on the installed Swift 6.1.2.

Purpose: This is the contract every later plan builds on (recipe model, attribute scale and families, ledger shape, reducer signature, save envelope). Proving it end-to-end first, on the agent's best context, catches an architectural dead-end after one commit.
Output: The BrewCore package with the tracer path, a minimal style classifier, the recipe rules (validation floor D-07, grain-bill auto-rebalance D-14, hop and conditioning edit rules, day cost D-10), and tests.
</objective>
